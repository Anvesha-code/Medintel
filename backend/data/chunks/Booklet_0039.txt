9. Allotment:
9.1 
The allotment of ﬂats shall be made through a lottery in the presence of the applicants, and the information regarding the 
same shall be published separately in newspapers and on the Authority’s website.
9.2 
The results of the lottery shall be publicly displayed on the Authority’s notice board and on the Lucknow Development 
Authority’s website.
9.3 
After the lottery, digitally signed allotment letters can be downloaded from the Authority’s website 
https://www.ldalucknow.i
