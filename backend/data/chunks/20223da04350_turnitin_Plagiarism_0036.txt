The Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) paradigm has emerged as a promising approach for enabling intelligent, context-aware information retrieval from vast, unstructured text data. Recent developments in Large Language Models (LLMs) have demonstrated remarkable capabilities in understanding and processing medical terminology, making them suitable for healthcare-specific applications. The RAG approach addresses the hallucination problem commonly associated with LLMs by grounding responses in factual, user-specific data.
