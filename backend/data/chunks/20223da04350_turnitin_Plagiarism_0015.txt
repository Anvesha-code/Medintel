At its core, the application securely stores vector embeddings of user-specific insurance and healthcare documents within Amazon Aurora PostgreSQL, utilizing the pgvector extension. This strategic choice allows for highly efficient similarity searches on large datasets of ML-generated embeddings. A crucial aspect of HealthAssist's design is its commitment to data privacy and security, achieved through row-level security in Aurora PostgreSQL, ensuring strict isolation of tenant embeddings in multi-tenancy workloads.
