11.2. Smoothing the Forecast Plan

He forecast plan will need to be realistic and achievable. This means that the forecast must not exceed engineer capacity, and that the forecast dates fall within the correct deployment windows. Also, the applications list and required by dates must be reasonable, and agreed with the application packaging team.
