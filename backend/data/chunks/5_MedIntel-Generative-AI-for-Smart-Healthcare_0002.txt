Challenges in Healthcare Data Management
Scattered Records
Medical data is fragmented across various sources and 
formats.
Format Diversity
Data exists in PDFs, reports, images, and prescriptions, 
complicating access.
Ineffective Search
Keyword-based search fails to capture medical context, 
leading to missed information.
Critical Information Risk
High risk of overlooking vital medical details due to 
disorganization.
The need for an intelligent, unified medical data system is clear.
