11. Readiness Review

On completion of the initial onboarding and validation stage, the process moves towards a readiness review. In this stage of the process, the user will be reviewed for suitability of moving to a T-30 target date for deployment, and the plan updated for users that cannot be targeted due to one or more of their readiness items being at risk.
