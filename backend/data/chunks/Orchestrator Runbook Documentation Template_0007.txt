Purpose:=nan, This template is designed to help you manage and properly document your Microsoft System Orchestrator Runbooks. We have compiled the items that are necessary for troubleshooting, migrating, and supporting your Orchestrator environment. It is intended to provided a concise, yet informative, view of your runbooks including all things they impact as well as things that could impact them such as description, creator, date created, modified by, date modified, reason modified, service accounts, connections to other applications, connections to other runbooks, required data to run successfully, email addresses used for notifications, schedules, and anything else that allows you to see what is critical to each runbook.
