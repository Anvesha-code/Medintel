There are four different application categorisations:

• Uncategorised (default)

• Keep – the application will be maintained in the new environment

• Retire – the application is not required in the new environment

• Forward-Path – the application is being forward pathed to either a new version, rolled up with other applications to a standardized version, or forward pathed to a functionally similar application

To set an application to ‘Keep’, simply click on the Keep radiobutton and click on update.
